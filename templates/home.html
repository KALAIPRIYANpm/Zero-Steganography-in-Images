Why DNA Cryptography?

Genetic Encoding

We transform binary data (0s and 1s) into synthetic DNA sequences (A, T, G, C), creating a biologically-inspired layer of obfuscation that baffles traditional sniffers.

Zero Steganography

Unlike traditional methods that hide data inside images, we treat the image itself as the message. We convert the raw binary of the file directly into DNA strands.

Two-Factor Decryption

Security is paramount. Decryption requires both the correct user session and a unique 8-character key sent via a separate channel (Email), ensuring military-grade protection.
